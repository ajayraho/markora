Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026

Markora is a Markdown reader and editor that runs in your browser. It's built so that your documents stay yours: there's no Markora account and no Markora database.

What stays on your device

Notes, your library, open tabs and settings (style, theme) are stored in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB and localStorage). Files you open from your computer are read and saved directly by your browser. If you turn on Google Drive sync, a copy of each Markdown file you open is kept in your own Drive so it's available on your other devices. Nothing is uploaded anywhere else.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose Connect Google Drive, Markora asks Google for these permissions:

  • drive.file: create and edit only the files Markora itself creates (a Markora folder holding copies of your notes and of the Markdown files you open in Markora). Markora cannot see any other files in your Drive.
  • openid and email: to show which Google account is connected.

Your notes travel directly between your browser and Google's servers. Markora's server never receives, reads or stores their contents.

To keep you signed in, a small server function exchanges Google's login code for a refresh token. That token is encrypted and stored only in a secure, HttpOnly cookie on your own device. It is not kept in any database. The server uses it only to obtain short-lived access tokens for your browser.

Markora's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your data is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone.

Disconnecting and deleting

Disconnect in Markora revokes its access and deletes the sign-in cookie. Your notes stay in your Drive and on your device until you delete them. You can also remove access at any time from your Google Account's third-party connections. Clearing your browser's site data removes everything Markora stored locally.

Analytics and cookies

Markora uses no analytics, trackers or advertising. The only cookies are the sign-in cookies described above, set only if you connect Google Drive. The hosting provider (Vercel) may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and request time, for security and operations.
